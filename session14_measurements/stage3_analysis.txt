  DOES ANY NON-ENERGY COLUMN MOVE? (non-C3 rows)
    145 paired non-C3 rows checked, 18 differ
      battery_drain        16 rows
      none                 2 rows

    WHY, AND BOTH REASONS ARE REAL FEEDBACK PATHS:
    battery_drain -- the coefficients are ~25 % lower, so the
      drained robot survives longer and the mission that
      follows is a different mission. Visible directly in
      robots_alive_at_end going 2 -> 3 on C5_s17 and C5_s18.
      Energy feeding back through battery exhaustion is the
      caveat sensitivity.py already flags.
    none (fault-free) -- C0 and C1 run detect=True and
      recover=True: they are the false-positive gates, not
      fault-tolerance-off arms. detection.py's PREDICTIVE
      battery check projects energy_j forward against
      remaining charge, so it crosses its threshold at a
      different STEP under different coefficients. The same
      false accusation fires (counts are unchanged: 5 across
      C0+C1 in both datasets) but at a different moment,
      shifting one claim release and therefore the routing.
      No reported C0 or C1 mean moves as a result.
